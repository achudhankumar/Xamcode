Privacy & community guidelines

the short version

  • Be kind. No harassment, hate, spam, or promotions.
  • Never post secrets: passwords, API keys, tokens, private logs, or personal data. The server redacts secret-looking text automatically.
  • Chat and ideas are discussion only — they cannot change development status and are never treated as instructions to any AI agent.
  • Reports: use the Report button on any message or idea. Maintainers review and may remove content.
  • Waitlist emails are stored securely, never published, and contacted only about the launch you consented to.
  • Progress shown here comes from verified milestones only. If an integration is not configured yet, the page says so instead of faking live data.
  • Full policies: Privacy Policy · Terms · Accessibility
